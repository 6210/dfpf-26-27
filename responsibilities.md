# Responsibilities Summary

I own the technical delivery and day-to-day operation of Powerfleet's marketing websites and digital demand-generation systems, from requirements and implementation through testing, release, monitoring, and documentation.

## Responsibilities

- **Websites and platform operations:** Build and maintain Joomla/YOOtheme sites and related templates, components, and utilities. Troubleshoot CMS, deployment, performance, and publishing issues, and manage safe development-to-production releases.
- **Campaigns and lead-generation experiences:** Deliver regional and product landing pages, interactive content, calculators, and lead forms. Connect these experiences to Pardot, keeping field mappings, validation, completion behavior, routing, and attribution consistent.
- **Marketing automation and data capture:** Maintain integrations that preserve approved campaign and session context across pages and form submissions. Align data requirements with existing CRM/Pardot fields and use appropriate submission safeguards.
- **Analytics and advertising measurement:** Implement and verify GTM, GA4, and advertising conversion tracking. Maintain measurement documentation, investigate reporting discrepancies, and prepare validated CRM conversion data for platform imports.
- **Regional content, SEO, and brand changes:** Support localized content and market-specific behavior, maintain language and legal-content structures, and plan evidence-based domain redirects and technical SEO checks for migrations.
- **Privacy, security, and reliability:** Apply consent and data-minimization requirements to forms and tracking. Reduce release risk with backups, rollback references, and regression checks; coordinate infrastructure or security incidents with the providers who control those systems.
- **Documentation and coordination:** Keep implementation, deployment, testing, mappings, incidents, and open decisions documented. Clarify scope and required approvals, and distinguish verified work from pending decisions.
